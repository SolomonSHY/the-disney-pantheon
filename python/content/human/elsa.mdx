Our final pairing is a gift to the readers. If the first 13 pairings didn't
convince you that there is a canonical match between the 13 Disney princesses and
the 13 Olympians, then the serendipity of this final one surely must. The most
famous Greek god who is technically not an Olympian, matched up against the most
famous Disney heroine who is technically not a Disney princess.

Hades is the god of the dead and the underworld. His realm is cold. A kingdom of
isolation, and it looks like he's the king.

Hades is Elsa.

The eldest male god (read, older sibling) is seen as orderly, lawful and fair, yet
feared due to his associations with death and the underworld. Likewise, Elsa is
the only Disney princess who is feared for her powers, although she is not evil.

In early drafts of Disney's Frozen, Elsa was written as a self-proclaimed Snow
Queen with blue skin, spiky black or blue hair, and an army of snow monsters, who
intentionally froze her sister's heart. Her concept art bears more than a passing
resemblance to Hades himself as portrayed in the 1997 Disney animated film
*Hercules.*[^conceptart]

[^conceptart]: Compare the early Elsa concept art by
[Claire Keane](https://x.com/claireonacloud/status/678669347770904580/photo/1) with
the [Hades concept art](https://disney.fandom.com/wiki/Hades/Gallery?file=Hades_concept.jpg).

Hades' most famous item is his Cap of Invisibility (kyneê), used in his first war
against his father, Cronus. Elsa's early childhood revolved around 'conceal it,
don't feel it', taught to her by her parents, manifested by gloves and the locked
door. Just as Elsa stopped concealing her powers after becoming queen, Hades
stopped using his Cap after becoming King of the Underworld. Finally, shared with
Demeter, Hades's most important myth is the abduction of Persephone, an allegory
of winter. Elsa takes on aspects of all major roles in that story, being
simultaneously the ruler of the cold realm, the one held captive, and the one who
caused the winter in Arendelle.

---

**Extra-diegetic.** Hades is not an Olympian on a technicality, because he doesn't
live on Mount Olympus. His other name is Plouton — the Rich One, derived from all
the earth's underground riches. It's why the Romans called him Pluto. Elsa,
similarly, is not a Disney princess, because Frozen makes so much money that Disney
presents it as its own standalone powerhouse.[^franchise]
