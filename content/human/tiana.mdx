We need a princess who works hard. Off to a good start, we've already eliminated
at least half the Disney princesses. Preferably, she would have an actual
profession or run a business. This princess would convert raw materials into a
finished product. And if at all possible, let's have her know her way around a
fire.

## Tiana, from the Princess and the Frog

Her kitchen is his forge. Her pot is his anvil. She runs a successful business.
The Mardi Gras ball is the most Hephaestan image in the canon. The whole
glittering court is in costume, and she is the one holding the tray, making
everything happen. And now let's hammer it in.

- Work ethic — Tiana works two waitress jobs
- Transactional relationship — She trades a kiss from Prince Naveen for a reward
- Hideous form — She turns into a frog. She's green and slimy. That form does not show up in promotional material for the film.
- Cast out — Tiana is chased out of New Orleans into the bayou.
- Success through labor — Tiana builds her dream restaurant by hand.

Most importantly, unlike other princesses whose happily ever afters imply a life
of ease and luxury, Tiana doesn't stop working even after the film credits roll.
The forge fires never dim.
