ARIEL! Ariel is far and away the most aquatic princess, and *The Little Mermaid*
drips with thalassic iconography. But look under the surface and there's no depth
to the connection. All Ariel wants is to *leave* the ocean — *"What would I give
if I could live out of these waters?"* That's a problem for the god of the sea.
Her whole arc is trading away her voice, taking a new form, and walking as a
stranger in a foreign land. It simply doesn't match Poseidon's vibe.

Well, if not Ariel, then… **Moana.** And here's why.

## Moana claims the sea; Ariel gives it up

With its maritime setting, Moana is off to a great start, and she beats Ariel
even on Poseidon's *lesser*-known domains:

- **Poseidon is god of horses.** He doesn't swim the ocean — he rides a chariot pulled by a hippocampus. Unlike Ariel, who swims, Moana has a chariot: her Polynesian outrigger canoe.
- **Poseidon is god of navigation** and patron of sailors. Moana's crowning achievement is the rediscovery of wayfinding across the open sea.
- **Poseidon is god of earthquakes;** his name means "master of the earth," and he was an earth-god long before a sea-god. Moana is the only princess who sees an island rise from the sea floor — literal land-creation — and descends to Lalotai by splitting the ground open.

Poseidon is wrathful, brooding, tempestuous; Moana is resolute, dutiful,
persistent — and both are implacable. Her arc is refusing to give up despite
enormous odds. There are even unsubtle nods to Poseidon's rivalry with Zeus, the
sky god: *"See the line where the sky meets the sea, it calls me."* In *Moana 2*
she thwarts Nalo, the god of storms (read: Zeus), by breaking his curse and
raising Motufetu.

Finally, the demigod in the room. Why does **Maui** loom so large? Why would a
prideful god share the limelight? Look at Maui's abilities: he held up the sky
(Atlas), stole fire from below (Prometheus), lassoed the sun (Helios), harnessed
the breeze (Aeolus), pulled islands from the sea (Poseidon himself), was cast out
at birth (Hephaestus), is a shapeshifter (Zeus) and a trickster (Hermes). Maui is
Maui.[^maui] And why bring in a god from *another pantheon* and paint him as your
best friend? Because you're a spiteful, temperamental god, antagonised by your
fellow Olympians, and this is how you get back at them: *"This is Maui, he can do
everything you guys can do and more, and we're best friends."*

[^maui]: Maui says it himself in his intro song: *"Yes, it's really me, it's
Maui."* Breathe it in.
