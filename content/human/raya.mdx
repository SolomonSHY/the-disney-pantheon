Ares is the god of war — though you'd be forgiven for mistaking him as the god of
*losing* wars. His rival Athena flattens him with a rock she picks up off the
ground. Two giants stuff him in a bronze jar for thirteen months. A mortal wounds
him and he runs home screaming. Hephaestus catches him naked in a net and invites
everyone to look.

Just as Ares is overshadowed by Athena — the god of war but better in every way —
we're looking for the *other* princess of war, the one people vaguely remember
after blurting out Mulan's name.

## It's Raya, from Raya and the Last Dragon

War defines the entire story. How are things when the film opens? Not good — the
tribes are at war. Why is Raya an orphan? War. Why is Sisu the last dragon? War.
Why are the five tribes of Kumandra at war in the first place? You guessed it.
The Kumandran war is the direct consequence of a frozen conflict between the
Dragons and the Druun that's been running for 500 years. A cosy setting for the
god of war.

Raya's headgear, the Salakot, is the tell. She's the only princess with
full-coverage headgear, and the film uses it three ways at once: weather
protection, disguise, and emotional armour. Ares' single most consistent
attribute across all iconography is his helmet. And unlike Mulan, Raya keeps her
helmet on. (Head to head, though, the smart money is on Mulan — just as Athena
beats Ares, you can bet Mulan pulls some environmental trickery to win the day.)

But how can a film that *ends in peace* be about Ares? Because *Raya* is a
retelling of perhaps the only myth where Ares plays a central role and comes out
ahead — the founding of Thebes:

- Cadmus finds a spring guarded by a water-dragon, a son of Ares, and kills it.
- He sows its teeth; from the furrows rise the Spartoi, the Sown Men. Cadmus throws a stone among them and each blames his neighbour. They fight until five are left.
- Cadmus serves the god eight years in penance, and at the end Ares gives him **Harmonia** — his daughter by Aphrodite — as a wife. Harmony, taken into the house, ends the strife. Thebes is founded.

Now Raya: a land literally shaped like a dragon; a last water-dragon, Sisu,
found at the river's tail; five factions — Fang, Tail, Spine, Talon and Heart —
sprung from a divided dragon, fighting over the gem for 500 years, each blaming
the others. Raya searches for six years, and at the end the tribes unite and
Kumandra is made whole. The outcome isn't a departure from Ares — it's his myth's
own ending. Peace comes only when the god of war lets go: Raya gives up her piece
of the gem and lets the Druun petrify her, so the others can unite.

---

**Extra-diegetic.** *Raya and the Last Dragon* took about $163M adjusted
worldwide — the lowest of any film in the princess canon. No song, no Oscar. It
is the least-seen princess film ever made, which is the most Ares thing in this
entire project. He is the god with no temples, no hymns, no festivals. His only
monument is a bare rock: the Areopagus, the Hill of Ares.
