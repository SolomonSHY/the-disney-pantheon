Hestia's name means "hearth, fireplace, altar" and she is the virgin Goddess of the
hearth and home. In classical Greek art, she is depicted as a woman simply and
modestly cloaked in a head veil. Hestia is famously dutiful, self effacing and
non-confrontational: she gave up her seat on the 12 Olympians to Dionysus. And yet,
Hestia is considered by some to be the oldest of the Olympians, and the first
recipient of any sacrifice.

We're looking for a princess who works hard around the home — cleaning, cooking,
sweeping. A nonconfrontational princess who serves others and gets her ass kicked
rather than kicks ass. If we were greedy, we'd also want her name to be associated
with the fireplace.

Finally, Hestia gave up the twelfth Olympian seat to Dionysus, the party god. Is
there a Disney princess famous for running away, as the clock strikes twelve, from a
party?

Hestia is Cinderella.

Objection! Hestia is a virgin goddess, doesn't that conflict with Cinderella's happily
ever after? No, because Hestia is a virgin purely by circumstance. Poseidon and Apollo
both wanted her; she swore an oath on Zeus's head to remain unmarried to avoid the
quarrel. She didn't get married because she had two competing suitors rather than
just one.

And in the film, Cinderella only gets married after the first slipper breaks and she
says: *"That's okay, I have another one."*

As the saying goes, if the shoe fits, wear it!

**Extra-diegetic.** Hestia is the Goddess of the home and customarily receives both
the first and the last portions of every sacrifice, meal, and libation. Cinderella
Castle is the centrepiece of Walt Disney World and appears in the Walt Disney
Pictures production logo.[^logo] Every Disney film released for decades opens with
the home of Hestia.
