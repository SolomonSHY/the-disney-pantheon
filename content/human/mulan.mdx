I'll come right out and say it: Athena is Mulan. The Greek goddess of wisdom and
strategic warfare, matched to the Disney princess of intelligent warfare. But
here are two lesser-known aspects of Athena.

**Athena is the patron of craft and weaving.** In the original *Ballad of
Mulan*, a Chinese folk poem dating to the Northern Wei dynasty (386–535 AD),
this is hinted in the very first line:

> 唧唧复唧唧，木兰当户织。
>
> *Tsiek tsiek and again tsiek tsiek, Mulan weaves, facing the door.*

Mulan is at a loom before she goes off to war — Athena the weaver becoming
Athena the warrior.[^arachne]

[^arachne]: When the mortal weaver Arachne beat Athena at the craft, the goddess
turned her into a spider, to weave for eternity.

**Athena was closely tied to her city.** Known as *Polias* and *Poliouchos*,
both from *polis.* In the founding myth of Athens she bested Poseidon for the
city's patronage by creating the first olive tree. Mulan's name means "flower of
the magnolia" — the official flower of Shanghai, the most important maritime
city in China.

Now the obvious parallels, in rapid succession:

- Athena is a virgin goddess, untouchable by Aphrodite. Mulan is one of the few princesses with no explicit romance arc.
- Athena preferentially disguises herself as men — Mentes, Mentor, Deiphobus, a young shepherd. Disguising herself as a man is Mulan's whole schtick.
- Athena is the only other god who can wield Zeus's thunderbolts, and has borrowed his aegis so long that he must borrow it back. Mulan, likewise, gets more mileage from her father's sword and armour than he ever did.

Finally, Athena is the goddess of strategic warfare. Mulan brings an avalanche
down on the Hun army — which makes her the undisputedly most lethal princess,
with a tally of 1,994 kills[^huns] and one assist.[^assist]

[^huns]: Disney animated 2,000 Huns in that scene; exactly six survived.

[^assist]: The assist is Shan Yu — the kill rightfully goes to Mushu.

**Extra-diegetic.** Athena's most famous plot is the Trojan Horse — conceived by
Odysseus, built by Epeius with Athena's craftsmanship, and left at a great
walled city as a gift. In 1998, the wall Disney needed to breach was the one
around China, after relations soured over the studio-funded *Kundun.* Their
plan: present Beijing with a movie. A movie about a girl whose name translates
as "flower wood-orchid," depicted riding a horse. A literal gift wooden horse.[^laocoon]

[^laocoon]: Athena, alas, failed to send sea-serpents to strangle the Chinese
Laocoön, and China did not accept the offering.
