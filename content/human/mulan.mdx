I'll come right out and say it: Athena is Mulan. The Greek goddess of Wisdom and
Strategic Warfare, matched to the Disney princess of intelligent warfare. But
here are two lesser known aspects of Athena:

Athena is the patron of craft and weaving.[^arachne] In the original "Ballad of
Mulan", a classic Chinese folk poem dating back to the Northern Wei Dynasty
(386–535 AD), this is hinted in the first line:

> 唧唧复唧唧，木兰当户织。
>
> *Tsiek tsiek and again tsiek tsiek, Mulan weaves, facing the door.*

[^arachne]: When a talented mortal weaver named Arachne beat Athena at weaving,
the enraged goddess turned Arachne into a spider to weave for eternity.

Mulan is weaving a loom before she goes off to war — Athena the weaver becoming
Athena the warrior.

Athena was closely associated with the city of Athens. She was known as Polias
and Poliouchos (both derived from polis, meaning "city-state"). In the founding
myth of Athens, Athena bested Poseidon in a competition over patronage of the
city by creating the first olive tree. Mulan's name translates as "Flower of the
magnolia tree", The magnolia is the official flower of Shanghai, the most
important maritime city in China.

And now, all the obvious parallels in rapid succession:

- Athena is a virgin goddess, untouchable by Aphrodite. Mulan is one of the Disney princesses with no explicit romance arc.[^bathing]
- Athena preferentially disguises herself as men: Mentes, Mentor, Deiphobus and a young shepherd. Disguising herself as a man is Mulan's whole schtick.
- Athena is the only other God who can wield Zeus's thunderbolts. She has also borrowed Zeus's aegis for so long that if Zeus needs it, he has to borrow it back. Similarly, Mulan has gotten more mileage out of her dad's sword and armor than her dad has.

[^bathing]: Aside: Mulan's comrades had no idea how close they flirted with
disaster during the bathing scene. The canonical punishment for seeing Athena
naked is instant blindness. Just ask Tiresias.

Finally, Athena is the goddess of strategic warfare. Mulan brings an avalanche
down upon the Hun army. Because of this feat Mulan is the undisputedly the most
lethal Disney princess with a tally of 1,994 kills[^kills] and one assist.[^assist]

[^kills]: We know this because Disney animators used 2,000 Huns in that scene, of
which exactly 6 survived.

[^assist]: Mulan's assist is that of the Hun leader, Shan Yu, as the kill
rightfully goes to Mushu.

**Extra-diegetic.** Athena's most famous plot is the Trojan Horse — conceived by
Odysseus, built by Epeius with Athena's own craftsmanship, and left at the great
walled city of Troy as a gift. In 1998, the wall Disney needed to breach was the
one around China.

Relations had soured after the release of *Kundun,* the Disney-funded biography
of the Dalai Lama that Beijing considered politically provocative. Disney was
looking to smooth things over and the plan they came up with was to present
Beijing with a movie. A movie about a girl whose name translates as "Flower
Wood-Orchid", depicted in both ancient texts and modern marketing material as
riding a horse. A literal gift wooden horse.[^serpents]

[^serpents]: Unfortunately for Disney, Athena failed to send two giant sea
serpents to strangle the Chinese equivalent of Laocoön and China did not accept
the offering. The Chinese government did allow the film a limited Chinese
release, but only after the Chinese New Year, so as to ensure that local films
dominated the more lucrative holiday market. Critical and audience reception in
China was negative overall, as they found its contents too foreign.
